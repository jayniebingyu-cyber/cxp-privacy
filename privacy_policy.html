Effective date: September 2, 2026

HEZHONG TONGHUI CO., LIMITED ("we", "us") operates the Chrome extension our local-only extensions. This policy explains what the extension does and what information it handles.

1. Local-Only Processing

This extension is a fully local tool. All processing happens inside your browser and no data is ever sent, uploaded, or transmitted to any server. We operate no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

2. Information We Collect

Nothing. We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, IP addresses, location, browsing history, or any personal information. The extension has no network access and no host permissions.

The extension requests only the minimal permissions needed to run (for example, storing your own settings locally). These are used solely to provide the requested feature on your device.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Any settings or data stored locally (such as your preferences) live only in your browser and are deleted when you remove the extension or clear your browser data. We hold no copy of any user data because no data is sent to us.

Our tools are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
