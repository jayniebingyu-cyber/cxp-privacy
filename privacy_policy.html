Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

HEZHONG TONGHUI CO., LIMITED ("we", "us") operates the Chrome extension our local-only extensions. This policy explains what the extension does and what information it handles.

1. Scope

This policy applies to extensions that operate fully locally with minimal permissions and no network access. They process content entirely inside your browser.

2. Local-Only Processing

This extension is a fully local tool. All processing happens inside your browser and no data is ever sent, uploaded, or transmitted to any server. We operate no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

3. Information We Collect

Nothing. We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, IP addresses, location, browsing history, or any personal information.

4. Permissions

The extension requests only the minimal standard browser permissions needed to run (for example, storing your own preferences locally). It uses only the standard capabilities of your browser and does not access any data beyond what is needed to display its interface and perform its function on your device.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Any settings or data stored locally (such as your preferences) live only in your browser and are deleted when you remove the extension or clear your browser data. We hold no copy of any user data because no data is sent to us.

6. Children

Our tools are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.
