Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

HEZHONG TONGHUI CO., LIMITED ("we", "us") operates the Chrome extension our AI-assisted extensions. This policy explains what the extension does and what information it handles.

1. Local-Only Core

This extension is a local tool: its interface and core processing run entirely inside your browser. We operate no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and we do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, IP addresses, or browsing history.

2. Optional AI Assistance (Your Own Key)

This extension lets you optionally configure your own API key from a third-party AI provider (such as an OpenAI-compatible service). When you use an AI feature, the text you deliberately select is sent to the API endpoint you configured to generate the result. The extension itself sends nothing to any server we control, and we never see your key or your text. If you do not configure a key, AI features are unavailable and nothing is transmitted.

3. Permissions

The extension requests only minimal permissions. It may declare a connection to the API endpoint you configure so your requests can be sent only when you use an AI feature. No permission is used to collect or transmit your personal information.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your key and settings are stored only in your browser and are deleted when you remove the extension or clear your browser data. We hold no copy of any user data.

5. Children

Our tools are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: support@hezhongth.com
