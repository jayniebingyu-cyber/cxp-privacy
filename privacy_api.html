Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

HEZHONG TONGHUI CO., LIMITED ("we", "us") operates the Chrome extension our online-data extensions. This policy explains what the extension does and what information it handles.

1. Local-Only Core

This extension is a local tool: its interface and most processing run entirely inside your browser. We operate no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and we do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, IP addresses, or browsing history.

2. Online Data Services

To provide its core function, this extension makes minimal network requests to well-known public services — for example currency conversion, dictionary lookup, weather, RSS feed retrieval, QR image generation, or translation. Only the minimal text or request needed for the function is sent to that service, which operates under its own privacy policy. The extension collects nothing beyond that request, and no data is ever sent to servers we control.

3. Permissions

The extension requests only minimal permissions needed to run, including network access to the public services described above. No permission is used to collect or transmit your personal information.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Any settings stored locally (such as your preferences) live only in your browser and are deleted when you remove the extension or clear your browser data. We hold no copy of any user data.

5. Children

Our tools are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: support@hezhongth.com
